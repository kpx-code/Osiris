— Osiris Deepnet KPX · autonomous trading AI · crypto & FX

Osiris trades.
And learns from every trade.

A self-directed trading intelligence for BTC, ETH and SOL. It scans the market every ten seconds, weighs its own probabilities, and guards every open position — on the Binance spot and futures testnets, with fake capital.

One principle drives it: stay honest about your own certainty. Every closed trade flows back into a calibration table that checks what the model predicted against what really happened, and the whole system is nudged by that truth. A raw score is trusted only as far as its measured win rate earns. Nothing changes silently — every self-adjustment is written to a live feed.

Osiris runs as two engines that share one brain: Osiris Neo for BTC / ETH / SOL, and Osiris Trinity (FX) — the same self-calibrating, session-aware intelligence applied to the foreign-exchange majors.

— Autonomous tools · Osiris tunes these itself

The toolkit

Each one is self-correcting: Osiris scores its result out-of-sample and shifts its weight, thresholds or allocation on its own. That is what the AUTONOMOUS tag means.

AUTONOMOUS

Predict

Calibrated per-coin direction forecaster. Every call is scored out-of-sample; the Governor decides how much of a vote it gets.

AUTONOMOUS

Governor

Self-governance. Gives every tool a trust weight that tracks its hit-rate and Brier score. Weak → scaled down, strong → scaled up.

AUTONOMOUS

Timing-Agent

Bundles NN, node countdown, FSO, RL, confluence, volume, fib & ETA into one readiness score for the entry moment. Live + shadow, each component self-weighted.

AUTONOMOUS

Timing shadow & backtest

A parallel timing agent plus a scenario backtest over ~600 candles. Thousands of entry scenarios seed the calibration; the shadow can promote itself once proven.

AUTONOMOUS

FSO zone-calibration

Stress zones (spanning / crisis / variance / correlation) recalibrate — but only after a shadow backtest and a live confirmation agree, in small bounded steps.

AUTONOMOUS

Kinetic breakpoint

Treats stored asynchrony as potential energy and the move as kinetic; predicts the nearest breakpoint, direction and ETA per coin.

AUTONOMOUS

FSO opportunity

Finds the real "lightning" chance per coin and tilts wallet equity toward the market where a big move is building.

AUTONOMOUS

DeepNet gate

Entry gate and live exit steering that Osiris switches on/off by walk-forward precision — with stall-protection so trades never freeze.

AUTONOMOUS

RL exit-agent

Tabular Q-learning over thousands of scenarios (HOLD / CLOSE / TRAIL / SCALE) steers discretionary exits and re-tunes the time-stop.

AUTONOMOUS

Guardian & Risk

Guardian catches frozen feeds and price divergence; Risk enforces drawdown, daily-loss and exposure limits — both pause entries before harm.

AUTONOMOUS

Margin engine

Futures with real testnet fills, autonomous leverage (3–5×) and a notional exposure cap that scales back on drawdown.

AUTONOMOUS

Self-review

Every 5 closed trades Osiris re-weighs recent data and adjusts exposure and thresholds — with the reasoning saved and readable.

AUTONOMOUS

Fee-aware guard

Compares real win rate to the win rate needed after fees. If costs eat the edge it raises the bar, cuts leverage and trades only top setups — never ramps up on gross win rate.

AUTONOMOUS

UOTAM node timing

Temporal attractor grid on the π-cycle (188.66 min, Tesla 3-6-9). Node countdown feeds the Timing-Agent and the fib confluence.

AUTONOMOUS

Predict inversion

When a predictor is reliably wrong, Osiris flips its signal — auto-detected from its own out-of-sample hit-rate, dual-track (fast + robust).

— Under the hood · the architecture

How the machine is built

Layers of intelligence stacked on one shared engine — each panel a real subsystem.

CORES

🧠 Four brains

Neo BTC + ETH + SOL cores, each self-calibrated; the Osiris mainbrain weighs them and routes to spot & margin.

STACK

▤ L1 · L2 · L3

Adaptive weights → Platt-calibrated logistic → DeepNet 16-14-8-1 per coin with a meta-gate that abstains when unsure.

REGIME

◱ Gaussian HMM

Baum-Welch + Viterbi label the market trending / volatile / compressed / calm — the whole stack adapts per regime.

TUNER

⇄ Shadow-backtest

Coordinate-descent over target/stop on the live buffer, optimizing Sharpe — a lightweight auto-tuner.

SYSTEM

◉ FSO stress

Market-as-a-system: per-coin stress + correlation-break + variance across 4 time scales. Danger zones recalibrate only after shadow + live agree.

INPUTS

⬡ 16 live signals

Per coin, per cycle: VFM, ER, DB, CHAOS, momentum, VOL-Z, RSI, EMA, CNN, FIB, NN, confluence, funding, L/S, BTC-corr, sentiment.

CAPITAL

◧ Spot + Margin

Two wallets, one brain. Spot on the testnet; futures with real testnet fills, autonomous 3–5× leverage and an exposure cap.

MEMORY

☁ Cloud & sessions

Learnings, models & calibration persist locally + sync to private Supabase. A headless worker keeps it alive; every run is archived per wallet.

LOOP

↻ Feedback loop

Every closed trade flashes back through the net — calibration, weights, thresholds, exits & stops all re-tune. Nothing changes silently.

— Osiris · Trinity (FX)

Osiris Trinity (FX)

Trinity brings the Osiris brain to the foreign-exchange majors. Same idea, different market: it ranks the pairs by live opportunity, trades the moment that fits the active session (Asia / EU / US / overlap), and scores every result out-of-sample so its confidence stays honest.

SESSIONS

◷ Session-aware

Each FX session gets its own brain-trust — win rate, AUC and reliability weighted by how many trades it has actually seen, so a thin session leans on the pool.

SYSTEM

◉ FX FSO

A stress oscillator across the majors reads calm / spanning / crisis and scales position size — and blocks fresh entries when the market turns disorderly.

CALIBRATED

◎ Self-calibrating

Predicted vs. measured win rate per session, the same calibration discipline as the crypto engine — raw score trusted only as far as it earns.

FLOW

◧ Capital & commodity flow

A live world map of where capital and commodities are rotating, feeding the FX neural-net view.
